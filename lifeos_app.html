What this is. A decision console on a verified chart: three ephemeris engines agreeing to 0.0026″, Sarvashtakavarga totalling its required 337, Shadbala with five exact components, independently corroborated by a separate toolchain to within 1.2 arcminutes. Area scores follow one published formula. Wealth figures are compound arithmetic. Confidence bands are comparative, not measured probability.

What this is not. A forecast. Astrology is not a validated predictive method. Nothing here claims an exam score, a disease, a lifespan, a specific spouse, or a guaranteed return. Where the chart contradicts itself — partnership especially, where the weakest house has the strongest planet as its lord — the contradiction is printed rather than resolved. Where a value is not reliably computable, it says DATA NOT VERIFIED.

Bottom line. The highest-leverage action in this entire system needs no astrology at all. The chart simply happens to agree with it, loudly, and more often than it agrees with anything else.
