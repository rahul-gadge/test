A · Method — and what I will not do

How probabilities here are produced

No chart knows a probability. A horoscope cannot tell you what fraction of CAT aspirants convert to a top IIM. So every percentage below comes from real-world base rates, and astrology contributes only the timing window. Where I have no defensible base rate, I say so instead of inventing one.

Also declined, permanently: exam scores, disease, lifespan, the identity of a spouse, guaranteed returns, and any net-worth figure presented as certain. Your own rules asked for this; I'd hold to it regardless.

Every prediction below required at least three of: natal promise (D1), dasha activation, divisional confirmation (D2/D9/D10/D24), strength (Shadbala/SAV), and real-world plausibility. Where they disagree, the confidence is marked down and the disagreement is printed.

J · Most likely life — the honest version

You asked me not to flatter you

Scenario B is the most likely outcome, not A. Base rates are unsentimental: most serious CAT aspirants do not reach a top-6 IIM; most capable analysts become competent mid-to-senior managers rather than partners or founders; most Indian professionals with rising salaries save 10–20%, not 40%.

The most probable version of your life is: a good MBA (possibly not the top tier), a solid corporate career in analysis or consulting-adjacent work, a salary that looks impressive and a net worth that lags it, a flat with a mortgage by your mid-thirties, and financial comfort without financial freedom. That is a genuinely good life. It is not the one you described wanting.

The three things that most reliably separate B from A are unglamorous and entirely inside your control: the savings rate from your very first salary, staying in one domain long enough to compound expertise, and leaving a public record of your work. None of them require a favourable dasha. All three are available on a Tuesday afternoon.

Two uncomfortable structural facts
  • Your chart is better at earning than keeping, and this is the most-repeated signal in it — weak 2nd house, weakest planet as its lord, no Dhana Yoga, three planets in the 12th. If you do nothing about it, high income will not become wealth.
  • Your best era arrives at 33, not 25. If you need to feel successful in your twenties, you will likely make an impatient decision — an early venture, a job hop for a small raise, leverage you cannot service — in a period your chart explicitly marks as the wrong one for it.
