The formula

score = 5 × (SAV bindus ÷ 28.1) × (house lord's Shadbala rupas ÷ its classical minimum), capped 1–10. 28.1 is the average bindu count per house (337 ÷ 12). 4 · Karmic themes

The central finding

Your Atmakaraka is Saturn — the highest-degree planet at 29°14′, and the same under both the 7-planet and 8-body reckonings. In Jaimini terms, that is the soul's chosen curriculum.

And Saturn is the weakest planet in your chart — last of seven at 3.77 rupas against a 5.0 minimum. It also rules your 2nd house (money) and sits in the 6th with Rahu. The subject you are here to learn is the one you are least naturally equipped for. That is not a curse; it is a workload, and it is unusually legible.

Theme 1 — Structure, patience, and the compounding of unglamorous effort

Immature version: resenting slowness; seeking shortcuts; treating discipline as something that should feel motivating. Mature version: building systems that work when motivation is absent. If mastered: the weak 2nd house stops mattering, because saving becomes structural rather than emotional. Action: one automated financial system and one automated study system, this month.

Theme 2 — Rahu in the 6th, Ketu in the 12th: earn it in the arena

Rahu in the 6th is one of its better placements — genuine appetite for competition and problem-solving. Ketu in the 12th pulls toward withdrawal, foreign things, and letting go. What life keeps teaching: your growth comes through contest, not through retreat — but you need real retreat to sustain the contest. Immature: oscillating between grinding and vanishing. Mature: scheduled recovery treated as part of the training, not a failure of it.

Theme 3 — Depth that must surface

Exalted Jupiter in the 8th, Mula lagna, Ketu in the 12th, Mars and Venus in the 12th. A great deal of your chart happens out of sight. The lesson is not to become extroverted — it is to leave evidence. Publish, submit, ship. If mastered: your 8.1 career score becomes real, because the empty 10th house gets filled by things you made.

Explain like I'm 5

The thing you are worst at — being patient, boring and consistent with money and effort — is the exact thing your chart keeps putting in front of you. Not as punishment. Because that is the one skill that unlocks everything else you want.

Your talent is already there. It is the container that leaks.
